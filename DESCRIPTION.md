This mod adds a functional kitchen to Minecraft. Use the Cooking Table to find out what you can cook with what ingredients you currently have available, and start cooking immediately through a single click.

The kitchen is modular and can be upgraded through additional blocks, such as cupboards for expanded storage, a sink for infinite water, or efficient cooking with the oven. Some people even went as far as capturing a Cow in a Jar to provide them with infinite milk!

This mod requires [Balm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/balm), which must be installed alongside the mod.

### ![Features](https://mods.twelveiterations.com/img/features-header.png)

- Multiblock Kitchens consisting of several components that each unlock new abilities
- Browse food recipes that you can create with the ingredients you have available
- #NoFilter book to simply show all food item recipes (no matter what ingredients you have available)
- Sorting options to see the most nutritious foods you can make
- Supports Pam's Harvestcraft as well as many other food mods
